# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This page is a short version. Where it is not clear, the full Covenant text
applies.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without
harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask when you are not sure.
- Respect other people's time. Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or other demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Publishing another person's private information without their consent.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may ban a person from the project
for behavior they judge harmful.

To report a problem, use the private reporting channel of this repository:
open the **Security** tab on GitHub, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and
say that it is a conduct report. This reaches the maintainers in private. No
email address is published. Reports are handled in private. A maintainer
who is part of the problem must not take part in the decision.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when a person speaks for the project in public.
